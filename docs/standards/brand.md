# CAMLEAP and ONEFOP: brand usage

**Status:** normative. Decided by the owner on 2026-10-12.
**Scope:** every surface of `react-web/` and the emails it sends. Official PDF exports are out of scope (see §Official documents).

---

## The relationship

ONEFOP (Observatoire national de l'emploi et de la formation professionnelle, under MINEFOP) owns the platform. CAMLEAP is the platform.

**CAMLEAP is the product; ONEFOP endorses it.** People do their work in CAMLEAP; the State speaks as ONEFOP.

## The name

The platform's name is written **CAMLEAP**, one word, in capitals, exactly as on the logo. Not CAM-LEAP, not CamLEAP, not Camleap.

This applies to the interface, the browser tab, emails and user-facing documentation. Code identifiers, file names and the repository may keep their existing spellings.

## The rule

> CAMLEAP where people do the work, ONEFOP where the State speaks, both together only at the front door.

| Context | Mark | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Front door**: landing, sign-in | **Both, as one lockup** | ONEFOP emblem, a thin vertical rule, the CAMLEAP logo, and under them "Une plateforme de l'ONEFOP" / "A platform of ONEFOP". Equal visual weight, read as one unit. |
| **Registration wizard** | **CAMLEAP alone** | The CAMLEAP logo in the side panel, with "Une plateforme de l'ONEFOP" / "A platform of ONEFOP" under it in words. One logo in the wizard (owner, 2026-10-15). |
| **Working screens**: questionnaire header, respondent home | **CAMLEAP only**, small | The CAMLEAP logo top left, 20px tall. ONEFOP appears once, as a line of text: under the logo ("Une plateforme de l'ONEFOP") where the screen has no footer, as in the questionnaire header; otherwise in the footer ("Plateforme de l'ONEFOP · MINEFOP"). |
| **Official and legal moments**: the questionnaire start notice, submission receipt, emails | **ONEFOP leads** | The ONEFOP emblem heads the notice as the issuing authority. CAMLEAP appears only as the opening beat or as "transmitted via CAMLEAP". |
| **Staff console** | **CAMLEAP**, "ONEFOP" as its subtitle | The rail is dark, so it uses the reversed (white) CAMLEAP logo. |
| **Browser tab, favicon** | **CAMLEAP** | Tab title "CAMLEAP". The favicon is a square CAMLEAP mark. |

**Never:**

- both logos side by side in a working screen's header — two marks competing for attention;
- the ONEFOP emblem as decoration on screens where ONEFOP is not speaking;
- the CAMLEAP logo on a background it does not read on (the navy "LEAP" disappears on dark: use the reversed logo);
- a recoloured, stretched, outlined or shadowed logo.

## Official documents

Official PDF exports (questionnaire copies, receipts generated by the backend) carry the ONEFOP / MINEFOP letterhead of the paper forms only. Changing them is an official-output change and needs explicit human approval (CLAUDE.md §21).

## Logo colours

The artwork's four inks are `--cam-logo-green` `#077440`, `--cam-logo-navy` `#021f40` and the two leaf accents `--cam-logo-accent` `#0e8445` and `--cam-logo-accent-light` `#58ad3b` (tokens.css). They are the logo's own colours and nothing else uses them: UI green is `--cam-green` `#1e6b3a`, a quieter green. The inline launch mark paints itself from these tokens; the static `.svg` carries the same values literally, since an `<img>` is never reached by the stylesheet.

The 2026-10-12 vector artwork shifted both inks slightly from the raster logo it replaced: navy `#001a3c` → `#021f40`, green `#006e3a` → `#077440`.

## Minimum sizes

The CAMLEAP logo is not shown below 16px tall. The vector wordmark is 7.09 times as wide as it is tall, so that floor is a width of 114px — which is what the one-line auth header sets (`.cam-brand-lockup--compact`, raised from 96px on 2026-10-12, where it had been drawing the mark 13.5px high). Leave clear space around each mark of at least half its height.

**The ONEFOP emblem is not shown below 56px** (`--cam-brand-emblem-min`, decided by the owner on 2026-10-12; this section said 24px until then). It is a seal, not a logo: ring lettering around a magnifier and a globe. Below 56px the lettering is an illegible smear, and a crisp CAMLEAP wordmark beside a smudge reads as amateur — the opposite of what an endorsement is for. It was being shown at 48px in the registration rail and 32px in the one-line auth header.

**Where there is not room for 56px, the endorsement is carried by the words, not the emblem.** "Une plateforme de l'ONEFOP" at 12px is legible; the seal at 32px is not. The one-line auth header (`AuthHeader`, `.cam-brand-lockup--compact`) does exactly this: the CAMLEAP logo, then ONEFOP in words, and no emblem.

This floor is why the Assets table below asks for a simplified ONEFOP mark. Until one exists, no surface may show the seal small — it drops the emblem instead.
